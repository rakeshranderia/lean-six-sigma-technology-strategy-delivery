# Roadmap Prioritisation

## Scoring Scale
Use a consistent scale, for example 1–5.

## Criteria
| Criterion | Weight | Definition |
|---|---:|---|
| Strategic alignment | 20% | Contribution to agreed strategy |
| Business value | 20% | Financial/customer/operational value |
| Risk reduction | 15% | Reduction of material exposure |
| Dependency enablement | 15% | Enables other roadmap outcomes |
| Customer/user impact | 10% | Experience/service impact |
| Effort | 10% | Delivery effort/capacity requirement |
| Cost | 10% | Total investment requirement |

Adjust weights to suit the organisation.
